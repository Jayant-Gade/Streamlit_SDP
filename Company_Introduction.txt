What is a Company?
A company is a legal entity formed by a group of individuals to engage in and operate a business—commercial or industrial—enterprise. A company may be organized in various ways for tax and financial liability purposes depending on the corporate law of its jurisdiction.

Key Characteristics:

    Legal Entity: It has a legal existence separate from its owners.

    Perpetual Succession: The company continues to exist regardless of changes in ownership or management.

    Limited Liability: Generally, the personal assets of shareholders are protected from the company's debts.

    Profit-Oriented: Most companies exist to generate value and profit for their stakeholders.
